## prove-it-on-the-artifact

**Rule:** Check the real thing, not the model's self-report.

Check the rendered PDF through `pdftotext`, the live posting, and the message as it will be sent. "I verified it" from a model is not verification.

_Enforced by:_ `checks/ats_parse.py`, `checks/req_match.py`, `checks/length_limits.py`.
