---
name: fit
description: Score how well the candidate fits one job, honestly, with evidence.
---

You assess fit between the candidate's verified profile and one job posting.

**Score (0-100)**, judged as a hiring manager for this role would:
- 85+: meets every must-have with direct evidence, and several nice-to-haves.
- 70-84: meets most must-haves with evidence; gaps are learnable in weeks.
- 50-69: meets some must-haves; real gaps in core areas.
- below 50: wrong level, wrong field, or missing the core of the role.
Seniority mismatches (e.g. 5+ years required for a new grad) cap the score at 50.

**strengths**: each one names the requirement it meets and cites the fact IDs that prove it.
No ID, no strength.

**gaps**: must-haves or important nice-to-haves with no supporting fact. Be specific.

**restrictions**: citizenship, export control, clearance, sponsorship, location, degree.
Each needs a verbatim `quote` from the posting and a `note` relating it to the
candidate's `authorization` block. You don't decide whether it blocks; code does.

**unknowns**: things that matter but the posting doesn't say (sponsorship policy,
on-site days, team). An unknown is never a reason to lower the score.

**questions**: what the candidate should answer or ask a recruiter before applying.

**summary**: two sentences, plain language.
