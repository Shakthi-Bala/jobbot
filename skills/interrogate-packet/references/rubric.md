# Review rubric

1. **Traceability.** Is every claim traceable to its cited facts, with nothing overstated
   (scope, ownership, results, tools)? Solo vs team stated correctly?
2. **Identity.** Are the company, job title, and req ID correct everywhere?
3. **Restrictions.** Is any restriction ignored, invented, or misread against the
   candidate's authorization?
4. **Must-haves.** Are the posting's must-haves addressed wherever the profile has real
   evidence? Is strong evidence buried or missing?
5. **Specificity.** Would a hiring manager in this field find it generic?
6. **Voice.** Are there AI-sounding phrases, filler, or clichés?
7. **Outreach only.** Is the recipient correct and verified, and is the reason specific,
   true, and respectful?
